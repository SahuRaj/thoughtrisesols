Your research journey with us

From the first idea to a finished thesis

We guide you through each stage of research, so you always know the next step.

  1. Topic Selection

    Choose a topic that is genuine, interesting and relevant to your field. We help you explore broader areas, gather enough information and narrow it down to a topic that is feasible, unique and manageable.

  2. Problem Identification

    Review recent publications, journals and other scholarly sources to understand what has already been researched on your topic, and where the gap lies.

  3. Research Proposal

    Present and justify the need to study your research problem, and outline practical ways to conduct the study. We guide you on structure, clarity and approach.

  4. Research Paper Development

    Guidance on research design, methodology and statistical analysis, and on presenting your results clearly and concisely.

  5. Journal Publication

    Shortlist suitable journals in your field, including impact factor journals, understand their author guidelines and prepare your manuscript to meet them with editorial support.

  6. Thesis Preparation

    Thesis work is a long journey, generally 2 to 4 months. We work alongside you to shape ideas and research strategies and keep your thesis on track to completion.
